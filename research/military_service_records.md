# Military service records — research pass, 19 Nov 2026

`military_service_records.csv` covers the 144 people (after duplicate folding)
whose record carries a military marker or veteran flag. Each was searched on
FamilySearch by name, middle initial and birth year (±1). Every row names the
records it rests on. **Nothing here is in `data/` and nothing is on the site.**
It is for the owner's review.

## What the `match` column means

| value | meaning |
|---|---|
| agrees with marker | Civil War index card whose name, initial and unit agree with the unit cut on the stone |
| matches by birth date | a service record (VA Master Index, AEF deaths) whose full birth date equals the project's |
| matches by residence | service record with the right birth year and a Loudoun residence; the project has the year only |
| probable | service record matching name, initial, birth year and birthplace; no date to check against |
| possible | a record under the name exists but nothing ties it to this person |
| conflict | the index contradicts the stone (Aaron H. Beans, Charles F. Myers) |
| draft registration only | a draft card only. **Registering for the draft is not service** |
| not found / not found online | no record found; after 1912 the route is NPRC, St. Louis |
| not searched | born after 1945: nothing of that era is indexed online |

## Sources used

- United States, Civil War Soldiers Index, 1861-1865 (NPS index cards)
- United States, Veterans Administration Master Index, 1917-1940
- United States, World War I American Expeditionary Forces Deaths, 1917-1919
- United States, World War II Army Enlistment Records, 1938-1946 (NARA AAD)
- United States, World War II Prisoners of War, 1941-1945
- WWI and WWII draft registration cards (identity and residence only)

Muster rolls (1916-1939) were not used as evidence: the index carries no unit
or birth date, so a common name cannot be assigned.

## Things worth the owner's eye

- **Lynwood L. Payne** (LV0745) died in service, 16 Feb 1918, on the AEF
  deaths list.
- **Edwin F. Hoeltzel** (LV1241) was a POW, captured 16 Dec 1944 and held at
  Stalag IV-B; probable, the POW file has no birth date.
- **Lewis Jones** (PV0118): the stone reads CO B US C INFANTRY, a US Colored
  Infantry soldier, the only one in the military set. The regiment is not on
  the stone and the index cannot place him.
- **Aaron H. Beans** (HM0023) is indexed in 6th Va. Cavalry Co. K, not the
  8th Va. the stone gives.
- **Charles W. White** (LV0970): the index also holds a Union Charles W. White
  of the Loudoun Rangers; the stone's 8th Va. card is the one attributed.
